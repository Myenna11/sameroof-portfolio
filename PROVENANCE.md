# Provenance and contributor attribution

This is a presentation edition derived from a privately used Same Roof project.
It is not a new project with a fabricated development history.

The source branch contained 267 commits. The privacy filter retained 199 original
change boundaries. Commits made empty by removal of private-only material were
pruned; subsequent preparation fixes are ordinary new commits. Original author
and committer timestamps were retained, but hashes changed. The mapping back to
the private source is retained outside this repository.

Removed from all exported history: personal room profiles/personas, handover
notes, internal review correspondence/research notes, live workspace config and
its lock/backup, generated usage snapshots and personal execution transcripts.
Examples, comments, namespaces and commit messages received consistent identity
and deployment-placeholder substitutions. The root config was newly created as
a fictional mock workspace. Earlier historical revisions are not guaranteed to
remain independently runnable after removing their private configuration.

Development was AI-assisted. Contributor identities remain separate:

- The human maintainer retains their original Git attribution.
- `AI contributor A (Claude)` and `AI contributor B` are stable pseudonymous
  labels for the original AI collaborator identities; they are not additional
  human team members or names of commercial products.
- `Build account (original root author)` preserves unattributed machine-account
  commits without claiming they were authored by the human maintainer.
- Contributors whose commits only changed removed private material do not
  acquire replacement commits merely to preserve a count.

No contribution was reassigned to the maintainer. The MIT license is retained.
Third-party runtime and library names refer to their respective projects; no
affiliation or endorsement is implied.

The original repository remains private and unchanged. Its CI runs, deployment
history and URLs are not proof of validation for the rewritten edition. Run the
checks here, and establish fresh CI results when publishing this repository.
